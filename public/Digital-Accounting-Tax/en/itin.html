Your ITIN, without mailing your passport.

An ITIN is the number the IRS issues to people who must file taxes but don't qualify for a Social Security number. As a Certifying Acceptance Agent, we verify your documents in our office, prepare Form W-7 and send it with your return. Your passport stays with you.

Who needs one?

  • People who work and must file but don't qualify for a Social Security number.
  • Spouses and dependents listed on your return.
  • Anyone with an expired ITIN who must renew it before filing.
  • Non-residents with U.S. income or investments.

What to bring for your ITIN

Check the list before your appointment. Document verification happens in person.

  • Valid passport, or two IRS-accepted identity documents
  • Birth certificates for minor dependents
  • For dependents, proof of U.S. residency such as school or medical records
  • The documents for your tax return

The exact list depends on your case. We'll confirm it on WhatsApp.

ITIN questions

How long does the IRS take to issue an ITIN?

Usually about 7 weeks, longer during tax season. We let you know as soon as there's an answer.

My ITIN expired. What do I do?

An ITIN expires if it isn't used on a federal return for three years in a row. We renew it before filing your return.

Does an ITIN let me work?

No. An ITIN is for tax purposes only. It doesn't authorize work or give Social Security benefits.
