What should I bring?

Pick your service and check the list before your appointment.

  • Valid photo ID
  • Social Security number or ITIN for everyone on the return
  • W-2 and 1099 forms from every job
  • Form 1098 if you pay a mortgage
  • Childcare costs, with the provider's tax ID
  • Last year's return
  • Bank account and routing number for your refund
  • Company formation documents and your EIN
  • Bank statements for the full year
  • Income records and invoices you issued
  • Business expense receipts
  • Payroll and contractor payments (1099)
  • Mileage if you drive for work
  • Last year's business return
  • Valid passport, or two IRS-accepted identity documents
  • Birth certificates for minor dependents
  • For dependents, proof of U.S. residency such as school or medical records
  • The documents for your tax return

The exact list depends on your case. We'll confirm it on WhatsApp.
