Accessibility

Our commitment to making this site usable for everyone.

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Draft for review: an attorney should review this text before it is published.

Our goal

We want everyone to be able to use this site, including with a screen reader, a keyboard only or enlarged text. It follows the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, level AA.

What we did

  • Enough color contrast in text and buttons.
  • Full keyboard navigation with visible focus.
  • Clear heading structure and text alternatives.
  • A complete version in English and Spanish.
  • No animation when your device asks for reduced motion.

If something does not work

If you find a barrier, tell us and we'll get you the information another way while we fix it.
