Questions people ask us

How much does a website cost?

We quote each project after we know what you need. Send us a short request and we reply with a written, itemized quote, usually within two business days.

Do I own the website?

Yes. The domain, the content and the design are yours. If you ever want to move on, we hand everything over.

How long does it take?

A one-page site usually takes about two weeks once we have your photos and text. Bigger sites take three to six weeks.

I don't have photos or text. Can you help?

Yes. We write copy in English and Spanish, and we can help you get photos that show your work well.

Can you fix my current website instead?

Often, yes. Put the link in the quote form and we'll tell you honestly whether fixing it or starting fresh makes more sense.

Do you work with businesses outside New Jersey?

Yes. We work remotely with clients in all 50 states, over video calls, email and shared drafts.

How do payments work?

Projects start with a 50% deposit and the rest is due at launch. Ongoing services are billed monthly. We accept card, ACH bank transfer and Zelle, and we send an invoice for every payment.

About us

A small studio that treats your business like its own.

We started Skybridge after building Stylo, when we noticed how many small businesses still lose customers to a website that won't load on a phone, or to a phone that rings while they're busy with someone else.

Our background is in engineering, so we like things measured, written down and done right the first time. When you work with us you get a clear price, regular updates and a straight answer when you ask a question.
